# 🚗 RoadResQ — Roadside Assistance & Emergency Support

> A smart roadside assistance platform that connects stranded drivers with nearby mechanics and roadside service providers using location-based services.

## 📌 Problem Statement

Vehicle breakdowns in remote or unfamiliar areas can leave individuals stranded without quick access to mechanics or roadside assistance. Finding reliable help can cause delays, inconvenience, and safety concerns.

The objective of this project is to build a web-based roadside assistance platform that quickly connects customers with suitable nearby mechanics and keeps them informed throughout the assistance process.

The solution focuses on GPS-based location tracking, real-time assistance updates, service-provider reliability, digital payments, and protection of user information.

## 💡 Our Solution — RoadResQ

**RoadResQ** is designed as a centralized roadside assistance platform connecting:

- 👤 Customers
- 🔧 Mechanics / Roadside Service Providers
- 🛡️ Admin / Operations Team

Instead of requiring a customer to manually search for a mechanic, RoadResQ is designed around a location-aware service request workflow.
